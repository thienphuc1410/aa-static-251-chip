Understanding the Difference Between HD, Full HD, and 4K

Standard-definition video at approximately 480p can be acceptable on smaller screens. HD video at 720p provides greater detail, while Full HD at 1080p generally offers a sharper image on laptops, monitors, and televisions.

4K Ultra HD increases the pixel count substantially, although its visible benefits depend on screen size, viewing distance, compression, and the quality of the original recording.
